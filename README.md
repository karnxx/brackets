Brackets is a fast-paced multiplayer game built around one rule: don’t trust anyone. Players battle inside a mysterious house where the lights constantly black out, creating tense moments of uncertainty. Read your opponent, bluff your intentions, survive the darkness, and outsmart them before they outsmart you.

# HOW TO SETUP A GAME
- first you neeed to install playit.gg
- after installing that, make a new server and note down its ip and port. (for example, play.asd.com:15125, here the play.asd.com is the ip, and 15125 is the port. remember to note these down WITHOUT the http/https protocol.
- then download the zip from the itch link and extract and run the exe.
- after running the exe, open playit.gg and start hosting (for only hosts)
- share the ip and port with players
- for players, just put the ip and port there and click on join, dont spam click cuz its in the process of joining.

# HOW TO PLAY
- a-d to move side to side and e to chat.
- after all players have joined click on the button on top
